TRUST & GOVERNANCE

Integrity is not a
claim. It is a system.

JAYERA makes consent, evidence, quality controls, independent roles, no-double-counting, farmer value sharing, and buyer transparency operational.

LAUNCH-READY CONTROLS

The controls behind credible outcomes.

These are product features, not policies hidden in a document. JAYERA should surface them in the relevant workflow at the moment a person needs them.

FARMER RIGHTS

Consent, land/plot rights, and benefit sharing.

Store agreement version, consent scope, participation terms, data permissions, benefit-share formula, and a visible grievance route.

EVIDENCE GOVERNANCE

Provenance, privacy, and corrections.

Record source device, time, location, photo metadata, editor/reviewer, version history, correction reason, access scope, and retention policy.

CLIMATE INTEGRITY

Method, baseline, risk, and reversals.

Track methodology, monitoring plan, uncertainty, survival, leakage, loss events, buffer/replacement approach, and requantification history.

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE

Separate roles. Scoped evidence.

VVB reviewers receive a structured, read-only evidence room, samples, findings, corrective actions, and signed decision trail.

ROUTE EXCLUSIVITY

One active issuance route per outcome.

Lock each vintage/outcome to a registry or ledger path; reconcile project/batch identifiers and retain cancellation or bridge records.

BUYER CLAIMS

Clear asset state and retirement proof.

Show available, issued, sold, retired, and cancelled states clearly; pair every buyer claim with its relevant evidence and status.
